    How catching works

    • The map is split into areas about 5 km across. The app checks the live weather for yours.
    • You can catch each kind of weather once per area, every day. Move to a new area, or wait for the weather to change, to find more.
    • Catching is a quick game: scoop rain or snow, hold to fill a jar with fog, time a tap for sun and wind, or tap the instant lightning flashes. From Uncommon up, weather can slip away if you miss.
    • Points: Common 10, Uncommon 25, Rare 75, Epic 200, Legendary 500. Points level you up, and levels unlock nearby weather, the forecast and early warnings.
    • Catch the weather, stay put, and be there when it turns, and you earn a Change moment like Sunbreak.
    • Sky Wonders like the full moon come from the sky calendar. Rainbows, halos and the Northern Lights are caught with a photo.
    • The weather data can miss a passing shower or a patch of fog. If you can see weather the app hasn’t picked up, tap Spot something. The app checks the weather around you first.
    • Catch the same weather 5 times for a silver jar and 15 for gold.
    • Shelf rewards: every 10 jars unlocks a new wood or lights for your shelf, with bonus points. Finish a row and that shelf changes for good. Get every jar in a row to gold and the shelf turns gold. Fill the whole shelf to earn the Sky Jar.

    Drive mode

    Switch it on before you set off and put your phone in a holder with the app open. It catches the weather on your journey into sealed jars, with nothing to touch. Open them when you’ve stopped. Passengers can play as normal.

    Add it to your home screen

    It opens like an app, and your jars are safer there.

    Back up your jars

    Your jars live on this phone. Copy a backup code and keep it somewhere safe, or paste one to bring jars back. Photos aren’t included.
